## Boyayyen Rikicin Riba: Me Ya Sa Tsohon Tsarin Rarraba Kaya Guda-Guda Ke Gazawa

Bari mu faɗi gaskiya game da yadda tsohon tsarin rarraba kayan ƙararrawa yake a aikace, domin fahimtar rauninsa shi ne matakin farko na magance shi.

A tsohon tsarin, mai rarraba kaya yana samun kayan ƙararrawa daban-daban — PIR motion sensors, door contacts, vibration detectors, sirens, da kwamitin sarrafawa — daga masana'antu daban-daban a ƙasashe daban-daban. Kowane mai kawo kaya yana da lokacin isar da sako (lead times), adadin saye mafi ƙanƙanta (MOQ), da ma'aunin inganci daban-daban. Mai rarraba kaya da ke ajiye kaya iri talatin daga masu kawo kaya takwas daban-daban ba gudanar da kasuwanci yake yi ba; gudanar da rikicin yau da kullum yake yi.

Wane ne ke ɗaukar nauyin wannan matsalar? Yawancin lokaci sunan mai rarraba kaya ne yake lalacewa, idan ma ba ribarsa ba ce ke raguwa kai tsaye.

Suna son cikakken tsari wanda aka gwada kuma yake da garantin aiki. Wannan yana nufin cewa masu rarraba kaya waɗanda ke sayar da sassan kaya kawai ana wuce su ana komawa ga masu samar da cikakken tsarin tsaro.

Yi la'akari da wannan ta fuskar riba. Mai rarraba kaya da ke sayar da PIR sensors guda-guda a kan ribar kashi 12–15%, sannan ya sayar da panel daban, yana samun riba mafi ƙanƙanta.
