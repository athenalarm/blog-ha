Ga shugabannin saye, zaɓar masu samar da ƙararrawar ɓarayi da ke riƙe da kuma ci gaba da sabunta takaddun shaida masu dacewa yana haifar da ƙima mai auna:

## II. Muhimman Takaddun Shaida da Kowane Mai Samar da Kayan Ƙararrawar Ɓarayi Ya Kamata Ya Riƙe

1. [ISO 9001](https://www.iso.org/iso-9001-quality-management.html) – Tsarin Gudanar da Inganci

Menene shi:
ISO 9001 yana tabbatar da cewa tsarin gudanar da inganci (QMS) na mai samarwa ya cika ƙa’idojin duniya da aka amince da su, waɗanda ke mai da hankali kan daidaiton tsari, ci gaba mai ɗorewa, da gamsuwar abokin ciniki.

Dalilin muhimmancinsa:
Ga masu saye a yawa, ISO 9001 yana nuna tsauraran hanyoyin masana’antu. Masu samarwa masu ISO 9001 yawanci suna nuna:

* Daidaitattun hanyoyin gwajin aiki.

* Ingantaccen bin diddigi a dukkan matakan samarwa da sauye-sauyen ƙira.

* Ƙananan ƙimar lahani da ƙarfi a amincin aiki.

Tasirin aikace-aikace:
Ƙungiyoyin saye za su iya tsammanin ingancin samfur mai tabbas, wanda yake da muhimmanci ga manyan shigarwa inda daidaito a cikin ɗaruruwan na’urori yake zama muhimmin abu.

2. [CE Marking](https://ec.europa.eu/growth/single-market/ce-marking_en) – Daidaituwar Turai

Menene shi:
Alamar CE tana nuna cewa samfura sun cika dokokin Tarayyar Turai na lafiya, tsaro, da muhalli, ciki har da dacewar lantarki da tsaron ƙaramin wuta.

Dalilin muhimmancinsa:
Alamar CE wajibi ce don sayar da tsarin ƙararrawar ɓarayi a Yankin Tattalin Arzikin Turai (EEA).

Tasirin aikace-aikace:
Masu saye da ke niyya kasuwar EU suna kauce wa jinkiri a kwastam da matsalolin alhaki, domin samfuran da ke da alamar CE sun riga sun wuce cikakken binciken doka. Wannan yana da muhimmanci musamman ga kwamitocin ƙararrawa masu waya da marasa waya da na’urorin haɗi.

3. [Ka’idojin UL](https://www.ul.com/services/standards-certification) – Takaddun Shaidar Tsaro na Arewacin Amurka

Menene shi:
Ka’idojin Underwriters Laboratories (UL) suna daga cikin mafi girmamawa wajen tabbatar da tsaro ga kayan lantarki da ƙararrawa a Amurka da Kanada.

Ka’idojin UL masu alaƙa sun haɗa da:

* UL 681 – Tsarin ƙararrawar ɓarayi.

* UL 827 – Ayyukan tashar tsakiya na ƙararrawa.

Dalilin muhimmancinsa:
Takaddar UL tana ba da tabbaci mai zaman kansa cewa tsarin ya cika tsauraran buƙatun tsaro na lantarki da na aiki.

Tasirin aikace-aikace:
Masu saye a Arewacin Amurka suna amfana da rage kuɗin inshora da kuma rage haɗarin doka yayin shigar da kayan da ke da takaddar UL.

4. [Takaddar FCC](https://www.fcc.gov/engineering-technology/electromagnetic-compatibility-division/equipment-authorization) – Dacewar Lantarki (Amurka)

Menene shi:
Hukumar Sadarwa ta Tarayya (FCC) tana tsara na’urorin mitar rediyo. Wannan ya haɗa da na’urorin watsawa da karɓa na ƙararrawar ɓarayi mara waya.

Dalilin muhimmancinsa:
Amincewar FCC tana tabbatar da cewa sadarwar ƙararrawa mara waya ba ta tsoma baki da sauran muhimman tsarukan sadarwa ba, kuma tana bin iyakokin fitar mitar RF.

Tasirin aikace-aikace:
Masu saye da ke shigar da tsarukan ƙararrawa na GSM/4G/Wi-Fi a Arewacin Amurka suna samun tabbacin cewa sigina za su yi aiki bisa doka kuma cikin kwanciyar hankali ba tare da tsangwama ba.

5. [EN 50131](https://standards.iteh.ai/catalog/standards/clc/3f3d3e83-2b0a-4a5b-9a2a-9b3b5a0d4f6e/en-50131-1-2006) – Ka’idar Ƙararrawar Kutse da Fashi ta Turai

Menene shi:
EN 50131 cikakkiyar ka’idar ƙararrawa ce da ake amfani da ita a duk Turai, wadda ke fayyace matakan daraja (Mataki 1–4), hanyoyin gwaji, da ma’aunin aikin tsaro.

Dalilin muhimmancinsa:
Matakin daraja yana nuna kai tsaye juriyar haɗari da aikin tsaro.

Tasirin aikace-aikace:
Ga shigarwa masu matuƙar muhimmanci—kamar cibiyoyin bayanai, filayen jiragen sama, ko cibiyoyin kuɗi—tsaruka masu manyan matakai (misali Mataki 3 ko Mataki 4) suna ba masu saye cikakken tabbaci cewa kayan aiki sun cika tsauraran ka’idojin aiki.

6. Ka’idojin Muhalli – [RoHS](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/topics/waste-and-recycling/rohs-directive_en) & [REACH](https://echa.europa.eu/regulations/reach/understanding-reach)

Menene su:

* RoHS yana takaita amfani da abubuwa masu haɗari a cikin kayayyakin lantarki.

* REACH yana tsara sinadarai da amintaccen amfani da su a cikin Tarayyar Turai.

Dalilin muhimmancinsu:
Bin ka’idojin muhalli yana ƙara zama wani ɓangare na manufofin saye na kamfanoni da ke mai da hankali kan dorewa.

Tasirin aikace-aikace:
Masu saye da ke bin manufofin muhalli, zamantakewa, da shugabanci (ESG) suna amfana da haɗin gwiwa da masu samarwa waɗanda ke rage amfani da abubuwa masu haɗari a cikin sassa da hanyoyin masana’antu.

7. [CCC](https://www.ccc-mark.com/) – Takaddar Tilas ta China

Menene shi:
CCC takaddar tsaro da inganci ce ta tilas a China.

Dalilin muhimmancinsa:
Masu kera da ke samarwa a China dole ne su cika ka’idojin CCC don sayar da kaya a cikin gida, kuma sau da yawa don nuna bin doka a sarkokin samarwa na yankin Asiya-Pasifik.

Tasirin aikace-aikace:
Masu shigo da kaya na duniya, musamman waɗanda ke sayen kaya a yawa daga China, suna ganin bin CCC yana taimakawa wajen sauƙaƙa bin ka’idoji a yankin Asiya-Pasifik.

## III. Bambance-Bambancen Yankuna a Ka’idoji da Kalubalen Bin Doka

Turai

* Tsarin doka ya haɗa da CE, EN 50131, da dokokin kariyar bayanai (misali [GDPR](https://gdpr.eu/)) inda tsarukan ƙararrawa ke haɗuwa da cibiyar sadarwa ko ayyukan gajimare.

* Bayan Brexit, [UKCA marking](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/using-the-ukca-marking) yana maye gurbin buƙatun CE don shiga kasuwar Birtaniya.

Arewacin Amurka

* Ka’idojin UL da FCC suna ayyana mafi yawan tsarin doka, tare da ƙarin dokokin gini da tsaro na jihohi da na ƙananan hukumomi.

* Masu saye a Kanada su tabbatar da daidaiton [ULC](https://canada.ul.com/ulcstandards/) (Underwriters Laboratories of Canada).

Asiya-Pasifik

* Takaddun CCC na China da [TELEC](https://www.telec.or.jp/ENG/) na Japan suna da muhimmanci musamman ga samfuran da ake amfani da su a waɗannan yankuna.

* Kasuwanni masu tasowa kamar Indiya na iya buƙatar [BIS](https://bis.gov.in/) (Bureau of Indian Standards) ko makamancin haka.

Ƙoƙarin Daidaituwar Duniya

Ƙungiyoyin ƙasa da ƙasa kamar [International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC)](https://www.iec.ch/) suna ƙirƙirar ka’idoji da nufin daidaita hanyoyin gwaji, rage maimaita bincike, da sauƙaƙa bin doka tsakanin ƙasashe.

## IV. Hadari da Kuɗin Rashin Bin Ka’idoji

Rashin bin ka’idoji ba matsalar doka kaɗai ba ce; matsala ce ta aiki da kuɗi:

* Hadarin doka: Tarar kuɗi, tilastaccen kiran dawo da kaya, ko haramcin kasuwa a manyan yankuna.

* Cikas ga aiki: Jinkirin ayyuka sakamakon tsare kaya a kwastam.

* Lalacewar suna: Illar suna idan shigarwa ta gaza ko ta karya ka’idojin yankin.

Ga ƙungiyoyin saye, binciken takaddun shaida tun da wuri yana hana tsadar gyare-gyare bayan an kammala shigarwa.
